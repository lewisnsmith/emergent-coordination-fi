# AI use

AI assistants may help with code, tests, documentation, literature discovery, and adversarial
review. The human author remains responsible for the research question, scientific design,
execution approvals, source checking, interpretation, and publication decisions.

AI output is not evidence. Citations, model and dataset claims, licenses, numerical results, and
statistical conclusions must be checked against their underlying sources or reproducible
artifacts. Generated code is reviewed and tested under the same standard as human-written code.

Public study reports disclose material AI assistance accurately. Internal chat transcripts,
temporary workflow narration, and empty contribution templates are not publication artifacts.
Prompts or model outputs are released only when doing so is lawful, scientifically useful, and
consistent with their licenses and privacy constraints.
