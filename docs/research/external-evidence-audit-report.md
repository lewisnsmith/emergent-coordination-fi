# External evidence audit

**Snapshot:** `external-evidence-2026-08-12`

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-08-12 23:59 America/Los_Angeles

**Status:** frozen broad open-evidence decision audit

This snapshot covers H1–H13, H2b, exp-000–exp-026, and the public-dataset,
owned-alpha, and validated-risk tracks. It establishes which claims are already occupied, which
methods and artifacts can be reused, which contradictions the designs must preserve, and which
evidence remains indispensable to collect.

The audit does not change a study contract, hypothesis, budget, publication claim, or registered
decision rule. It found no artifact that currently passes the gate for a `partial_substitute` or
`full_substitute`, so it authorizes no experiment-cell reduction.

## Decision summary

The program should stop positioning generic LLM investment convergence, generic LLM market
bubbles, generic prompt effects, rationale unfaithfulness, pressure sensitivity, or low-bit
reasoning degradation as first demonstrations. Those territories are occupied.

The strongest remaining contribution is narrower and better identified:

- a matched `technology × ecology` comparison of LLM and strong classical strategies;
- component-level MPHIQ decomposition of model, profile, harness, information, and wording;
- delegation breadth at a fixed real-investor pairwise-convergence anchor;
- a paired, randomized AI-capital-share response in a validated reconstructable exchange;
- finance-specific paired input and activation interventions with causal controls;
- an orthogonal financial pressure design with safety, suitability, abstention, quantity, and
  convergence outcomes;
- same-checkpoint precision by dependency-depth, context, and state-feedback propagation with a
  separate local-to-frontier behavioral bridge.

External evidence changes emphasis and sequence. It saves method development and prevents weak
claims, but it does not remove the owned identification needed for the central questions.

Two downstream questions remain externally blocked:

- **H7:** no open source supplies a repeatable time series of verified AI or LLM investment
  discretion, autonomy, affected assets, exposed capital or order flow, model lineage, and a stable
  denominator.
- **H10:** no identified study combines independently verified deployment, quantified exposure,
  credible assignment or natural variation, and a real-market outcome with the required
  falsification tests.

H9 may proceed as simulation discovery and carefully labeled real-domain resemblance. Without
known target labels, it cannot claim real calibration, exposure, or causation.

## What was audited

The audit unit was a question or estimand by experiment cell by output-track row. A paper-level
label was insufficient because one work can provide a method for one row, a prior for another, and
no evidence for a third.

The protocol used four evidence rings:

- `direct`: empirical evidence close to the registered construct;
- `correlated`: evidence about a relevant mechanism, comparator, moderator, or neighboring domain;
- `enabling`: statistical, simulation, interpretability, benchmark, or release methods;
- `horizon`: provisional, indirect, or discovery evidence that should not support a conclusion.

Permitted uses were `method_only`, `prior_only`, `partial_substitute`, and `full_substitute`.
Population, treatment or exposure, comparator, estimand, outcome, highest independent unit,
lineage, domain, dependence, multiplicity, leakage, missingness, negative controls, external
validation, artifacts, and component rights were assessed separately.

The search covered 21 coordinated lanes:

1. LLM trading and investment
2. algorithmic monoculture and behavioral fingerprints
3. investor herding measurement
4. market simulation and exchange validation
5. algorithmic collusion
6. crowding, liquidity, fire sales, and cascades
7. profiles, personas, suitability, and simulated-agent validity
8. human trust, advice use, and delegation
9. adoption measurement and forecasting
10. prompt pressure, forced action, authority, and adversarial framing
11. black-box attribution and rationale faithfulness
12. activation interventions and mechanistic transfer
13. local-to-frontier equivalence
14. quantization and error propagation

## Novelty map

### Occupied territory

The audit found direct precedent for each of the following broad claims:

- LLM investment recommendations can overlap and concentrate capital.
- Homogeneous and selected mixed LLM markets can exhibit model-specific aggregate behavior.
- LLM-agent markets can form bubbles or change simulated prices and liquidity.
- Prompt interventions can alter model financial behavior and simulated-market outcomes.
- LLM agents can operate in persistent limit-order-book or trading environments.
- Shared algorithmic components can homogenize downstream decisions.
- Autonomous pricing algorithms can learn supracompetitive outcomes in some institutions.
- Investment recommendations can collapse onto a small subset of stated profile features.
- Generated rationales can omit causal prompt influences.
- Pressure and forced-choice formatting can change performance and safety behavior.
- Low-bit quantization can harm hard reasoning tasks and propagate early errors.
- Financial reasoning performance declines with complexity and dependency depth.

These claims belong in the motivation and prior sections, not in a first-demonstration claim.

### Open or incompletely occupied territory

Under the frozen search protocol, no direct precedent was identified for:

- the full H1 matched technology-by-ecology estimand with strong classical strategy families and
  independent replay blocks;
- the full MPHIQ Hamming-edge component decomposition;
- AI-delegation breadth at fixed real-investor pairwise convergence;
- a paired randomized AI-capital-share threshold in a validated reconstructable exchange;
- a finance-specific paired causal input and activation-intervention program spanning action,
  safety, suitability, quantity, convergence, and held-out transfer;
- a finance-specific orthogonal stakes-by-urgency-by-emotion-by-forced-action design;
- same-checkpoint precision by executable financial dependency depth, context, and state feedback
  with first-error survival, recovery, closed-loop divergence, and causal mechanism transfer;
- a locked randomized-simulation AI signature validated and calibrated on a real market with known
  AI labels;
- real-market AI causation supported by verified deployment, quantified exposure, and a credible
  counterfactual.

This is a bounded search result, not a claim that no source exists.

## Contradictions and moderators to preserve

### LLM market behavior is not uniformly stabilizing or destabilizing

Henning reports relatively low-dispersion, often fundamental LLM markets. Saxena reports
family-specific bubbles and a selected mixed ecology with variable macro outcomes. Their model
generations, institutions, prompts, temperature, memory, contamination, and top-level replication
differ. Treat family by institution by ecology as a moderator, not one paper as a universal answer.

### Homogeneity depends on the metric

Recommendation and factor-style concentration can coexist with repeated-prompt variation and
cross-family multiplicity. Measure action agreement, portfolio similarity, strategy fingerprints,
breadth, and performance separately. Rationale-text similarity remains diagnostic only.

### Herding is not automatically harmful

Average institutional herding can be small, and buy herding can aid price discovery. Sell herding,
forced flows, leverage, illiquidity, and stress can create larger transitory distortions. H5 must
preserve direction, asset liquidity, quality, stress, persistence, leverage, funding, and forced-flow
moderators.

### High spreads are not automatically collusion

Punishment-based supracompetitive learning is one mechanism. Weak feedback or insufficient
exploration can also prevent agents from learning competition. Use collusion language only with
strategic response, profitable joint deviation, and punishment evidence.

### Aggregate realism does not validate cognition

Budget constraints and exchange rules alone can produce high efficiency. Declining fundamental
values and confusion can produce bubbles. Stylized facts alone do not validate an agent-based
model. The exchange must pass institutional, mechanism, response, and held-out validation.

### Pressure effects are two-sided

Pressure and emotional cues produce positive, negative, and null effects across tasks and models.
Forced choice can worsen safety even when a performance metric appears decisive. H12 therefore
needs two-sided tests, equivalence decisions, and safety noninferiority rather than a one-direction
story.

## Economic application

### Safe savings

The following changes reduce cost without weakening identification:

- reuse LSV and Sias measures with structural and activity-process sensitivity rather than inventing
  another investor-herding statistic;
- implement ODD documentation, established limit-order-book realism metrics, constrained
  zero-intelligence controls, and published strategic diagnostics;
- use established momentum, reversal, market-making, volatility-targeting, and statistical-arbitrage
  definitions for strong classical baseline families;
- inspect QFinZero and ABIDES/Get Real before building duplicate order-lifecycle, logging, replay, or
  realism infrastructure, but only after exact license and runtime review;
- use Gemma Scope on exact supported checkpoints and AtP* only as a discovery prefilter, then spend
  compute on a small held-out set of real interventions;
- use W8 as a cheap canary, W4 as the main quantization stress, and W3 only after a registered trigger;
- keep KV-cache quantization separate so a serving optimization does not confound the weight-precision
  estimand;
- reuse FinQA and ConvFinQA as ecological bridges only after a pinned smoke run, while retaining a
  private executable family-split scoring key;
- cache frontier outputs and query frontier endpoints only for finalists selected without their
  outcomes;
- narrow H12 to the orthogonal four-factor core and move authority, social proof, injection, and
  conflicting mandates into held-out stress families;
- use established equivalence, cluster, dyadic, multiplicity, reality-check, deflated-Sharpe, and
  probability-of-backtest-overfitting methods rather than creating replacements.

### Unsafe savings

Do not:

- delete the classical ecology because LLM-only markets exist;
- treat human experimental markets, professional advice, and 13F panels as interchangeable;
- count agents, calls, steps, pairs, symbols, or prompt variants as independent replication;
- treat a direct LLM paper as a validated H5 simulator or a substitute for randomized AI share;
- treat recommendation overlap as sequential convergence or capital exposure;
- infer collusion from agreement, bubbles from AI alone, or causation from a transported signature;
- use public benchmark scores as proof of contamination-free financial reasoning;
- use rationale text, SAE labels, one-way patches, or representation similarity as causal mechanism
  evidence;
- remove a collection cell before a named artifact passes provenance, comparability, rights, smoke,
  independence, leakage, and validation gates.

### Recommended spending order

1. Freeze strong classical baselines, activity matching, independent units, and exact endpoint
   lineage.
2. Run the cheap H13 same-checkpoint scoring-key ladder and staged H8 discovery.
3. Smoke-test the small set of external artifacts proposed for reuse.
4. Calibrate the exchange, reconstruct the book, and validate background liquidity before paying for
   broad H5 frontier replicas.
5. Run a small explicit bridge replication against the strongest direct LLM precedent.
6. Expand provider and robustness cells only after the first four gates pass.
7. Defer H7 and H10 confirmatory claims until verified exposure data exists.

## Output-track consequences

### Public research datasets

Established standards reduce documentation design work but do not prove the release. Use W3C PROV
entity, activity, and agent lineage; DataCite 4.7 identifiers, versions, rights, and relations; a
data card; tier-specific public and restricted manifests; per-source licenses; privacy threat
models; checksums; signed builds; held-out utility; and deprecation or retraction procedures.

The potential contribution is not another dataset card. It is a release that preserves simulation
truth, transported resemblance, verified exposure, and causal attribution as separate tiers and
links each claim to rows, transformations, code, and hashes.

### Owned alpha

The external audit does not register the missing alpha estimand. Before this track runs, an
outcome-blind amendment must specify the locked signal, all attempted variants, point-in-time data,
temporal splits and embargo, costs, slippage, turnover, capacity, factor exposure, multiplicity,
stop rules, and verdict criteria.

Use White's Reality Check, deflated Sharpe, probability of backtest overfitting, multiple-testing
controls, and prospective paper trading. Keep every failed or abandoned signal. Alpha can begin
only from a separately frozen rule after transport; it never proves AI exposure or causation.

### Validated risk

Risk claims must stay on an evidence ladder:

1. randomized simulation truth;
2. held-out simulation transport;
3. real-domain resemblance;
4. verified operational exposure;
5. causally attributed real-market effect.

Every result stops at its strongest passed tier. Official scenario reports and model-risk guidance
inform controls, not observed causal effects. The Federal Reserve's 2026 revised model-risk guidance
is useful only by analogy here because it explicitly excludes generative and agentic AI.

External evidence should inform outcome-blind amendments to these contracts. It must not silently
fill them in after results are visible.

## Snapshot verification and limitations

This is a broad, multi-agent, open-first evidence decision audit. It is not a claim of exhaustive
PRISMA-level retrieval. The audit used public scholarly, publisher, proceedings, regulator,
standards, repository, and artifact sources. Reviews were used for discovery, not empirical
conclusions.

Limitations that remain visible in the ledger:

- no institutional Scopus, Web of Science, EconLit, or PsycINFO exports;
- no subscription-only full texts beyond material openly available through authors or publishers;
- no complete raw OpenAlex export or PRISMA-S database export preserved in the repository;
- no full non-English database search or human-verified translation program;
- no author contact;
- no clean-environment smoke reproduction or full empirical reproduction;
- incomplete licenses and raw outputs for several direct 2025–2026 working papers;
- endpoint drift and nondeterminism for closed provider models;
- inaccessible material remains unresolved rather than negative evidence.

The frozen snapshot is therefore suitable for design, novelty, cost, and governance decisions under
its stated scope. Before submission, update the high-velocity AI lanes monthly, the remaining lanes
quarterly, execute reproducible database exports where access permits, perform two-wave citation
chasing, resolve the contact queue under separate authorization, and dual-screen every new direct,
contradictory, novelty-relevant, or substitution-relevant record.

The required novelty wording is: **“no direct precedent identified under the frozen protocol
through 2026-08-12.”**
