# 05 — Follow-up Questions

Secondary questions, ordered by proximity to the primary result. Experiment IDs below are
planned; only exp-000, exp-001, exp-002, and exp-010 currently have configs.

| # | Question | Planned test |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Within- vs cross-family convergence.** Does Claude agree with Claude more than GPT/Gemini, and can trades reveal a foundation-model fingerprint? | Provider-blocked replay cohorts and a decision-stream provider classifier (exp-003). |
| 2 | **Capability scaling.** Does convergence rise from small to frontier model tiers or with reasoning effort, distinguishing “smarter agents find the same optimum” from a shared prior? | Tier-blocked sweeps (exp-004). |
| 3 | **Temperature and sampling.** How much decorrelation does temperature buy, and at what performance cost? | exp-005. |
| 4 | **Persona/demographic sensitivity.** Which risk-tolerance, horizon, demographic, and mandate instructions change strategy rather than style, and how much dispersion can prompts restore? | exp-006. |
| 5 | **Information differentiation.** Do different news subsets decorrelate agents more than personas (H4), and what is the substitution rate between information and prompt heterogeneity? | exp-007. |
| 6 | **Memory and context.** Does trade memory increase convergence over time through reinforcement or reduce it through path dependence? | exp-008. |
| 7 | **Regime dependence.** Is convergence stronger in crises through a flight to the same safety than in calm regimes? | Compare synthetic regime blocks and historical windows (exp-009). |
| 8 | **Shared-market amplification (H5).** At what LLM capital share do cascade frequency, depth, or price dynamics change? | Sweep LLM-held capital from 0% to 100% (exp-011). |
| 9 | **Tacit coordination/collusion.** Do shared-market LLM market makers widen spreads supra-competitively without communication? | Calvano-style exp-012. |
| 10 | **Contamination.** Do pre-cutoff, post-cutoff, and synthetic behavior differ in ways consistent with memorization? | Planned robustness battery for exp-001. |
| 11 | **Advisor mode.** How much convergence survives when LLM advice passes through heterogeneous human executors with noise and latency? | No config assigned. |
| 12 | **Cross-market consistency.** Do the same models drive convergence in equities and prediction markets, or does market structure dominate? | No config assigned. |
| 13 | **Detection.** Can public-tape herding signatures or order timing reveal an LLM cohort to market surveillance? | No config assigned. |
