# Exp-008 MPHIQ exploratory screen

## Question

Which combinations of model, profile, harness, information, and semantic prompt sameness are
associated with decision convergence across the complete 32-cell MPHIQ design?

## Design

Cross the five binary sameness factors only after the replay and profile-information studies have
published frozen registries and checksummed outputs. Every scheme must exist, same-factor cells
must be byte-identical where claimed, different-factor cells must satisfy their declared contrast,
and cohort size and capital remain fixed.

## Estimands, units, and controls

Report exploratory main and interaction effects, paired Hamming-distance-one contrasts, and
hierarchical false-discovery-adjusted intervals. The independent unit is a non-overlapping window
or independently generated trajectory assigned to a complete scheme block; response seeds are
nested. Balanced zero bits and verified one bits are the primary design controls.

## Failure rules and limits

Any missing scheme, failed hash claim, factor imbalance, or unregistered analysis prevents a
release. The screen cannot confirm H1, H3, or H4 and cannot block their focused publications. H12 is
removed: semantic prompt sameness is not evidence about stakes, urgency, emotion, or forced action.

## Execution sequence

1. Verify the two upstream releases and freeze all five factor registries.
2. Materialize and audit all 32 schemes before any model execution.
3. Freeze exploratory contrasts, FDR handling, and independent units.
4. Gate provider calls, compute, spending, and output rights.
5. Run once without promoting discovered effects to confirmatory status.
6. Release all screened effects with explicit exploratory labels, including null results.
