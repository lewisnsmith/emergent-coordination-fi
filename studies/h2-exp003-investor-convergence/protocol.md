# H2 matched investor convergence

## Question

Is convergence among the sampled AI cohorts unusual relative to real-investor panels measured on
comparable outcomes?

## Design

Harmonize universe, cadence, activity, sampling, and outcome definitions before opening the
headline comparison. Consume only the checksummed AI convergence release from the replay study.
Construct matched non-overlapping panel periods and preserve source revisions and exclusions.

## Estimands, units, and controls

Estimate the matched difference in overlap and herding. The independent unit is a matched,
non-overlapping panel period. Report hierarchical intervals plus sensitivity to matching rules,
sampling filters, panel source, cadence, and activity. Balance reports and unmatched descriptive
summaries are mandatory controls.

## Failure rules and limits

Unresolved rights, inadequate overlap, failed balance, unstable matching, or incomplete panel
periods prevents the comparison. Unmatched 13F or trader panels cannot establish that AI is more
convergent than real investors generally. This study is descriptive, not causal.

## Execution sequence

1. Verify the upstream AI artifact and freeze the matching protocol.
2. Resolve investor-data provenance, lawful use, acquisition, and release rights.
3. Build the panel without inspecting the headline contrast.
4. Audit balance and freeze exclusions and sensitivity analyses.
5. Run once and release positive, null, or negative matched comparisons identically.
