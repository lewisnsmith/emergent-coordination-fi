# H6 human delegation

## Question

When do people choose AI advice or autonomous AI management under incentive-compatible conditions?

## Design

Randomize oversight, explanations, performance evidence, autonomy, and risk in approved choice
tasks. Human-adviser conditions, order randomization, and attention checks are controls. No
participant may be contacted and no data may be collected before ethics, consent, recruitment,
compensation, privacy, and storage approvals are complete.

## Estimands, units, and controls

Estimate participant-level randomized effects on delegated share. One consented participant is the
independent unit; repeated choices are nested and intervals cluster by participant. Freeze
randomization, exclusion, nonresponse, multiplicity, missingness, and attention-check rules before
opening outcomes.

## Failure rules and limits

Missing consent, protocol deviations, broken randomization, unsafe storage, or failed
deidentification blocks analysis and release. Model simulations cannot substitute for human data.
Claims are limited to the approved sample, tasks, incentives, and study setting.

## Execution sequence

1. Complete protocol, consent, sampling, tasks, privacy, and cost estimates.
2. Obtain ethics and separate contact, recruitment, compensation, and storage approvals.
3. Freeze randomization and analysis before enrollment.
4. Run balance, attention, nonresponse, and deidentification audits.
5. Release positive, null, or negative findings under the same participant-protection rules.
